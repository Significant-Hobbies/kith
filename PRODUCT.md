# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

One person who wants to remember the people in their life without treating
them like a contact list or a sales pipeline.

## Product Purpose

Kith keeps a small, private constellation of people and the notes that make
those relationships feel tended. Success is opening the app, seeing who is
close, tapping someone, and writing down what just happened — in under a
minute.

## Positioning

Kith is a warm personal log of people, not a CRM, not a contact book, and
not a social network. Closeness is something you set, not something the app
scores.

## Operating Context

- Used on an iPhone, often in a quiet moment after a call, dinner, or
  birthday, one-handed, with little patience for forms.
- The repeated action is "open someone, write a short note, leave."
- Data is intimate. It stays on the device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A person has a name, a circle (family, close, friends, work, other), an
  explicit closeness from 1 to 5, an optional birthday, how you met, standing
  notes, and a hue.
- Bubble size on the constellation is closeness only.
- Each person has a chronological log. An entry has a kind, a date, and a
  short body.
- The home surface is a floating constellation. A list with search exists
  for finding someone quickly and for VoiceOver.
- Everything is stored in one local JSON document. There is no account and
  no network request.
- Contact import, iCloud, notifications, photos, and a public site remain
  deferred.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Kith.
- Voice: warm, short, specific. Never motivational, never CRM.
- Core proposition: “The people you keep close.”
- Visual direction was delegated from the product brief: floating bubbles,
  size as relationship, warm UI.
